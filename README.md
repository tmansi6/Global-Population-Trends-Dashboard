# Global Population Trends Dashboard

## Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to explore global population dynamics across regions and income groups.

The project combines historical population data from 1960–2022 with projections for 2023–2050 to examine population growth, life expectancy, birth rates, death rates, and demographic change.

The dashboard is designed to support evidence-based exploration for policymakers, NGOs, and other users interested in population change, urbanisation, ageing populations, and regional disparities.

## Objectives

- Explore long-term global population trends.
- Compare demographic indicators across regions and income groups.
- Examine relationships between life expectancy, birth rates, and population growth.
- Compare female and male population levels over time.
- Provide interactive filtering for geographic and time-based exploration.
- Present demographic information through accessible, user-oriented visualisations.

## Data

The analysis covers:

- Historical observations: 1960–2022
- Future projections: 2023–2050
- Regional and income-group classifications
- Population measures
- Female and male population
- Population growth rate
- Life expectancy
- Crude birth rate
- Crude death rate
- Urban and rural population indicators

## Tools & Techniques

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Interactive slicers and filters
- Scatter charts
- Clustered column charts
- KPI cards
- Pie charts
- Regional hierarchies

Key DAX measures include average birth rate, average death rate, average life expectancy, average population growth rate, and total population.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Life Expectancy vs Birth Rate

A scatter visual compares average life expectancy with birth rate across regions over time, with population growth represented through bubble size.

### 2. Female vs Male Population

Clustered column charts compare female and male population levels across regions and selected time periods.

### 3. Demographic Comparisons

Additional visuals support comparisons of demographic indicators by region and income group.

### 4. Interactive Exploration

Users can filter the dashboard by:

- Region
- Time
- Income group
- Demographic indicators

## Key Insights

- Population patterns differ substantially across regions and income groups.
- Life expectancy and birth rates show an inverse pattern across several regional groups.
- Urbanisation is visible across many regions over time.
- Population growth rates vary considerably between regions.
- Birth and death rates provide evidence of different stages of demographic transition.
- The dashboard supports exploration of population ageing and potential population decline.

## Limitations

The dashboard is primarily an exploratory and descriptive analytical tool.

Although the underlying dataset contains projections through 2050, the dashboard itself does not implement advanced predictive or "what-if" modelling.

Interpretation can also depend on how users apply the available filters.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of Power BI, data modelling, DAX, and data visualisation to transform demographic data into an interactive analytical dashboard.

It combines high-level KPIs with regional comparisons and time-based exploration to make large-scale population trends easier to understand.
